METHOD

How Pressure Is Defined and Scored

Every delivery is tagged with a pressure index built from the factors named in the brief: required run rate, wickets remaining, balls remaining, innings stage and closeness of the contest.

Batting pressure index (per ball)

Classes: Negligible <0.10 · Low 0.10–0.30 · Moderate 0.30–0.50 · High 0.50–0.70 · Extreme ≥0.70. "Pressure" stats use High + Extreme balls only.
